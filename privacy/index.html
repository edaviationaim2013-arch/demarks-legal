Privacy Policy — Demarks Script API

Draft for review before publication. Last updated: November 22, 2026.

Demarks Ice & Spirits, Inc. operates Demarks Script API as an internal reporting integration. The application is intended to access accounting information from the QuickBooks Online company account that authorizes it.

Information processed

Subject to the permissions granted, the application may retrieve accounting records such as expenses, vendors, accounts, and transaction dates and amounts for internal reporting and reconciliation.

Purpose

The information is used to prepare internal operating reports, reconcile costs, and assess business performance. The application is not intended to sell QuickBooks information or provide it to advertisers.

Storage and access

Access credentials and accounting data may be processed on company-controlled computers or systems used to run the reporting integration. Authorized personnel should limit access and protect credentials. The final deployment configuration, retention period, and security controls must be verified before this policy is published.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate the integration or as legally required. Any actual sharing arrangements must be documented here before publication.

Disconnecting

The QuickBooks administrator may revoke the application's access through QuickBooks connected-app settings. Revoking access stops future API retrieval but does not automatically erase previously exported reports.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact Demarks Ice & Spirits through its established business contact channel. Add a monitored contact email before publishing.
